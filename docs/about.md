I’m an engineer with a B.Sc. in Electronics and Telecommunications Engineering and an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering (AI & Data Science), focused on computer vision and perception for robotics.

My work sits at the intersection of real-time vision, ROS-based autonomy, sensor integration (LiDAR/RGB-D/IMU/sonar), and deployment on constrained hardware. I care about end-to-end systems: data, training, evaluation, and what actually happens on the robot or edge device.

## Focus

- Computer Vision: object detection, segmentation, video analytics, deployment constraints
- Robotics: ROS/ROS2 perception stacks, SLAM, visual odometry, multi-sensor integration
- Edge AI: Raspberry Pi / embedded prototyping, real-time inference and monitoring
- Simulation: Gazebo/RViz; simulation-driven validation where appropriate
